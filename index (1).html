01 — The problem

Every city has the same broken minute.

↳

No live signal

Drivers navigate to an address, then are on their own for the part that actually costs them time.

↳

Fragmented data

Availability data exists across operators and cities, but nothing stitches it into one navigable layer.

↳

No community loop

Traffic apps solved this for roads by turning drivers into sensors. Parking never got the same treatment.

02 — The approach

We're not rebuilding the data. We're building what sits on top of it.

Parking data infrastructure licensed
Cruzlane navigation layer in development
Driver, in real time community input

The infrastructure to know where a spot might be already exists across dozens of providers and cities. What's missing is the consumer experience — a single, trustworthy, routed layer that feels as effortless as any modern navigation app.

Cruzlane licenses existing availability data and combines it with community-reported spots and predictive modeling, then routes drivers to a parking outcome the way a modern navigation app routes them to a destination.

Where we are today A working prototype exists and is in active internal testing. We haven't opened a public pilot yet — we're being deliberate about the markets and users we bring in first.
03 — Where we're headed
